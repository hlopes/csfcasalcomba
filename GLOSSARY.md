# csfcasalcomba

Institutional website for the Centro Social e Paroquial de Casal Comba. Content is data under `src/data/`; pages under `src/app/` pass it to modules under `src/components/`.

## Language

**Scroll reveal**:
A scroll-triggered entrance motion (fade plus a shift of at most 20px, plays once) applied to content entering the viewport.
_Avoid_: AnimateTop, AnimateLeft, AnimateRight (retired direction-specific module names)
